Professional Focus

My work is built on three principles guiding every publication or operational discussion: Observe, Understand, Improve.

Observe

Time-Critical Media Operations

Every operation leaves clues. More than four decades in broadcast, television, streaming, and digital media have reinforced one simple lesson: understanding a system begins by observing how it behaves under real-world conditions where timing, continuity, and sound judgment matter.

Understand

Operational Reliability

Reliable organizations are built through preparation, not luck. Reliability emerges from understanding how people, processes, technology, and decisions interact before failure occurs. It is a discipline of visibility, consistency, and continuous improvement.

Improve

Operational Visibility

Better decisions begin with better visibility. Through the Operational Visibility Series and the Reaction Cascade Effect, I examine how hidden risks, operational drift, and interconnected dependencies can be recognized before they become visible failures.

How engagements work

Most work begins with a brief discovery call to identify where workflows are breaking down or creating risk under real-time conditions.

  1. Discovery: goals, constraints, and operational context
  2. Review: workflow walk-through, dependency mapping, failure scenarios
  3. Deliverable: risk exposure summary + prioritized mitigation plan designed to reduce failure points and improve real-time response

Who this is for

Teams where time and accuracy matter—live events, automated playout, and environments where small failures escalate quickly and recovery must be immediate.

  1. Broadcast operations & master control environments
  2. Streaming teams supporting live programming or sports
  3. Organizations scaling distribution without mature runbooks
  4. Teams exposed to single-point-of-failure staffing or systems

About

Led by Randy Anthony, a broadcast and streaming operations specialist with decades of hands-on experience in time-critical environments. In these systems, failure rarely announces itself—issues surface quietly, compound quickly, and only become visible when timing is already compromised.

The focus is practical reliability: clarifying workflows, strengthening system visibility, and reducing the points where small problems turn into operational disruption.

The result is steadier execution, faster recovery, and systems that behave more predictably under pressure.

This perspective is grounded in real-world operations, including oversight of up to 19 simultaneous network feeds in live broadcast environments.
