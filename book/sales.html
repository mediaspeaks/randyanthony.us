Operational Reliability | Available Now

Reliability Is Not Improvised

Real-time systems reveal the truth. They expose the gap between how we believe things work and how they actually behave under pressure.

In Reliability Is Not Improvised, Randy Anthony introduces the Reaction Cascade Effect, an original framework explaining how small operational irregularities propagate through interconnected systems until they become visible failures.

Written by a broadcast and streaming operations professional with more than four decades working in time-critical media environments. The book grew from Randy Anthony's Operational Visibility work and the realities of operating systems where failure has immediate consequences.

For readers who want to understand:

  • Why small operational problems become major failures
  • Why systems behave differently under pressure
  • How instability becomes visible before disruption

Best for: broadcast, streaming, operations, engineering, management, and reliability-minded professionals.
