FM: The Fading Modulation
of Terrestrial Radio

Non-Fiction
Available Now

Radio didn't disappear. It evolved.

After the Telecommunications Act of 1996 removed ownership limits, consolidation, automation, and centralized programming transformed how stations operated. At the same time, digital platforms changed listener expectations, replacing shared experiences with unlimited choice and personalization.

In FM: The Fading Modulation of Terrestrial Radio, Randy Anthony examines how these parallel changes reshaped one of America's most influential media industries. More than a history of broadcasting, it is a thoughtful analysis of why radio sounds different today, what was gained, what was lost, and why the medium still matters.

Reliability
Is Not
Improvised

Operational Reliability
Available Now

Real-time systems reveal the truth. They expose the gap between how we believe things work and how they actually behave under pressure. In Reliability Is Not Improvised, Randy Anthony introduces the Reaction Cascade Effect, an original framework explaining how small operational irregularities propagate through interconnected systems until they become visible failures.

Drawing on more than four decades in time-critical media operations, this book reframes reliability as a discipline rather than a technical feature. It explores why systems drift, how failures accelerate, and how organizations can build resilience by recognizing instability before it becomes disruption.

Ella Umber & Her Umber-Ella

A Childrens Book
Available Now

Ella Umber doesn't think she needs an umbrella. The sky is bright, the sun is shining, and carrying one seems unnecessary. But when dark clouds roll in and a sudden rainstorm turns an ordinary day into a soaking adventure, Ella quickly discovers that being prepared isn't such a bad idea after all.

With a little help from her mom and plenty of muddy mischief from her playful puppy, Biscuit, Ella learns that unexpected surprises can lead to the biggest smiles. Filled with clever wordplay, colorful illustrations, and laugh-out-loud moments, Ella Umber is a heartwarming story about preparation, perseverance, and finding joy even on the rainiest of days.

Points South

Contemporary Romance
Available Now

Victoria King left town years ago with no intention of returning.

She built a life defined by control, precision, and distance from everything she once knew. But when she unexpectedly inherits Points South, the waterfront restaurant that shaped her childhood, she returns for one reason only: settle the business and leave for good.

Instead, she finds a place very much alive.

The restaurant is thriving. The town remembers exactly who she used to be, and every familiar street seems determined to pull her backward before she has the chance to decide who she wants to become.

Then there's Clark.

An outsider with a clear perspective, he understands the business, the difficult choices before her, and, increasingly, the woman hiding behind carefully constructed walls. What begins as practical conversation slowly becomes something deeper, forcing Victoria to question whether Points South is simply an unexpected inheritance...or the place she was always meant to come home to.

Points South is an emotionally layered novel of identity, unexpected connection, second beginnings, and the enduring pull of the places that never entirely let us go.
