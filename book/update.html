FM: The Fading Modulation of Terrestrial Radio

Industry Evolution
Available Now

An examination of how the Telecommunications Act of 1996 transformed American radio through consolidation, automation, changing business models, and technological disruption. More than a history of broadcasting, it is a study of how industries evolve, adapt, and sometimes lose the very characteristics that made them successful.

FM explores the operational, financial, and cultural forces that reshaped one of America's most influential media platforms, offering lessons that extend far beyond radio.

Operational Reliability
Available Now

Points South

Travel & Observation
Available Now

Victoria King left town years ago with no intention of returning. She built a life defined by control, precision, and distance from everything she once knew. But when she unexpectedly inherits Points South, the waterfront restaurant that shaped her childhood, she returns for one reason only: settle the business and leave for good. Instead, she finds a place very much alive.

The restaurant is thriving. The town remembers exactly who she used to be, and every familiar street seems determined to pull her backward before she has the chance to decide who she wants to become. Then there's Clark. An outsider with a clear perspective, he understands the business, the difficult choices before her, and, increasingly, the woman hiding behind carefully constructed walls. What begins as practical conversation slowly becomes something deeper, forcing Victoria to question whether Points South is simply an unexpected inheritance...or the place she was always meant to come home to.

Points South is an emotionally layered novel of identity, unexpected connection, second beginnings, and the enduring pull of the places that never entirely let us go.
