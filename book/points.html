About the Book

Victoria King left town years ago with no intention of returning. She built a life defined by control, precision, and distance from everything she once knew. But when she unexpectedly inherits Points South, the waterfront restaurant that shaped her childhood, she returns for one reason only: settle the business and leave for good.

Instead, she finds a place very much alive. The restaurant is thriving. The town remembers exactly who she used to be, and every familiar street seems determined to pull her backward before she has the chance to decide who she wants to become.

Then there’s Clark. An outsider with a clear perspective, he understands the business, the difficult choices before her, and, increasingly, the woman hiding behind carefully constructed walls. What begins as practical conversation slowly becomes something deeper, forcing Victoria to question whether Points South is simply an unexpected inheritance...or the place she was always meant to come home to.

Points South is an emotionally layered novel of identity, unexpected connection, second beginnings, and the enduring pull of the places that never entirely let us go.

The Story

At its heart, Points South is a story about returning to a place that remembers you, confronting the life you left behind, and discovering that coming home can mean becoming someone new.

A Contemporary Romance

The novel brings together family history, a living waterfront business, an unexpected inheritance, and a relationship that develops as Victoria is forced to reconsider the future she thought she had already chosen.

About the Author

Randy Anthony is an advisor, strategist, and veteran of radio, television, and digital media. His writing ranges across operational reliability, broadcasting, fiction, children's literature, and personal transformation. Points South explores a very different world from his operational work, but it reflects the same interest in people, choices, systems, and the forces that shape what happens next.
