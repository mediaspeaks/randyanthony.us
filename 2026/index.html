A Creative Toolbox on Your Phone or Computer

Think of SHIFT as a toolbox on your phone or computer that can write, design, and record things for you, simply by typing or talking in plain English. Need a flyer for your legal services business? Describe it, and it builds the image. Need a video explaining Legal Shield to a family in Charlotte? Describe it, and it makes the video, with no cameras, no editing software, and no design degree required.

SHIFT is a unified creation suite: image generation, video generation (single clips and multi-shot reels), voice cloning and text-to-speech, talking-avatar video, translation and dubbing, music generation, deck-building, and a chat assistant. These tools sit behind one account, one credit system, and one output destination: the Vault.

You're not stitching together five different subscriptions and manually re-uploading assets between them. Voice clones, saved characters, brand packs, and your own uploaded media are reusable across every studio.

You get a link for everything you create. You post that link. If people click it and buy something, you get paid a cut, and there are a few different ways that can stack up, not just one.
