One Creative Toolbox. Right on Your Phone or Computer.

Think of SHIFT as a toolbox that can write, design, and record things for you, simply by typing or talking in plain English. Need a flyer for your legal services business? Describe it, and SHIFT can create the image. Need a video explaining LegalShield to a family in Charlotte? Describe what you need, and it can generate the video, without a camera, editing software, or a design degree.

A unified creation suite

SHIFT brings a range of creative tools together under one account, one credit system, and one destination for your work: the Vault. Its tools include:

  • Image generation and video generation, including individual clips and multi-shot reels
  • Voice cloning, text-to-speech, and talking-avatar videos
  • Translation and dubbing
  • Music generation and presentation/deck building
  • A chat assistant

Instead of juggling separate subscriptions and manually moving assets between services, you can reuse voice clones, saved characters, brand packs, and your own uploaded media across the studios.

Your creations can help open doors

SHIFT says each creation includes an invisible attribution mark associated with its creator, designed to remain traceable even if the work is screenshotted. You also receive a shareable link for each creation. When people follow those links and make qualifying purchases, you may earn a commission. The platform describes multiple ways for those earnings to accumulate, subject to its terms and program rules.
