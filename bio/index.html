ABOUT RANDY ANTHONY

Operational Reliability Professional, Author & Time-Critical Media Operations Specialist

Randy Anthony is an author and operational reliability professional whose career spans radio, television, broadcast operations, digital media, and time-critical workflows. His work focuses on how people, technology, procedures, and decisions interact when an operation has to keep working under pressure.

Operational Reliability Making complex operations more dependable, visible, and repeatable.
Broadcast & Streaming Experience across time-critical media and digital distribution environments.
Author & Systems Thinker Writing about reliability, judgment, systems thinking, and operational consequences.

Professional History

Randy Anthony began his professional career in radio and television, learning early that technical failures become very public when the system involved is responsible for putting a signal on the air. That experience became a foundation for a career centered on reliability, operational discipline, and clear decision-making.

His career includes broadcast radio and television, voice and production work, digital editing, communications, and the evolution of media workflows from traditional broadcast into digital and on-demand environments.

Radio & Broadcast

Randy's early work in radio and television established the operational perspective that continues to shape his work today. His broadcast background includes on-air, production, and operational experience in environments where timing, continuity, and technical execution matter.

Digital & Streaming Operations

As media systems evolved, his work expanded into digital and on-demand distribution. Today his professional focus includes broadcast and streaming operations, workflow stability, operational visibility, and the practical decision-making required when systems are operating in real time.

Current Professional Focus

Randy works at the intersection of operational reliability, systems thinking, communications, and media. He examines where risk hides in real workflows, how small discrepancies can propagate through interconnected systems, and what organizations can do to make operations more resilient and understandable.

Operational Visibility & Frameworks

Randy is the creator of the Operational Visibility Series, a body of work examining operational reliability, human judgment, systems thinking, hidden dependencies, operational drift, and decision-making in time-critical media environments.

Related work includes the Operational Architecture series and the Reaction Cascade Effect, a framework developed through his work on how seemingly small technical or procedural discrepancies can produce larger operational consequences.
